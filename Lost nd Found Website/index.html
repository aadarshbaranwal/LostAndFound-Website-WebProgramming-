About Us

Lost & Found Portal is created to help people report and recover lost belongings easily. Our goal is to connect those who have lost items with those who have found them in a simple and reliable way.

This platform is designed for quick access, clean navigation, and user-friendly experience.

Who Are We

We are a team of students working together to build a real Lost & Found website for our college. Our aim is to create a digital platform where students and staff can report lost items and list found belongings easily.

Through this project, we are learning web development, teamwork, and problem-solving while trying to build something useful for real-life college situations.
